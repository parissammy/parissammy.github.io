Cash offers and wholesale acquisitions

We make cash, as-is offers on houses and close at a title company.

Flex Concierge Group LLC buys houses nationwide, with a focus on affordable single-family homes. Send us the property and, if it fits, we send a written cash offer. No repairs, no cleaning, no showings for us to schedule around.

Submit a property or email flexconcierge@gmail.com

How it works

  1. Send the property. Reply to our email or use the form below with the address and any details you have.
  2. We review it. We look at the price, condition, and local market.
  3. Written offer. If it fits what we buy, we send a written cash offer. If it does not fit, we will tell you.
  4. Inspection period. Once a contract is signed, there is a short inspection period.
  5. Close at a title company. The seller can choose the title company. Closing happens there, like any other sale.

Terms we typically offer

  • Cash purchase
  • As-is condition, no repairs requested
  • No earnest money
  • 10 to 21 day inspection period
  • 30 to 45 day close

These are typical terms, not a guarantee. Not every house will get an offer, and every offer, price, and timeline depends on the property and is set out in writing.

Where we buy

We buy nationwide, with a focus on affordable single-family homes. Listed properties, for sale by owner, estate, and as-is houses are all welcome.

Wholesaler disclosure

Flex Concierge Group LLC is a real estate investor and may assign its purchase contract to an end buyer. We are not always the end buyer of the property. We are buying our interest in the contract as a principal, not as an agent.

We do not represent the seller and we are not acting as a listing broker. Sellers and agents are welcome to have the contract reviewed by anyone they choose before signing.

Any offer we send is non-binding until a written purchase agreement is signed by both parties.
